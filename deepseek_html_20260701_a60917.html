FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Can I freeze my membership?expand_more
Yes, yearly members can freeze their membership for up to 3 months per year for travel or health reasons. Quarterly members are permitted 1 month of suspension.
Are personal trainers included?expand_more
Depending on your tier. Quarterly and Yearly members receive complimentary monthly sessions. VIP Elite members enjoy unlimited 1-on-1 coaching.
What is the cancellation policy?expand_more
We require a 30-day notice for all monthly membership cancellations. Multi-month plans are binding for the duration but can be transferred to others.
